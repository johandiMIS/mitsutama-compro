# Home Appliance Test and Equipment

## Overview

LISUN's household appliance testing solutions are fully compliant with **IEC 60335**, the
fundamental international safety standard governing household and similar electrical
appliances. The standard applies to equipment with a rated voltage not exceeding 250 V for
single-phase and 480 V for three-phase systems.

IEC 60335 sets stringent safety requirements and test methods spanning electrical,
mechanical, thermal, abnormal operation and electromagnetic compatibility domains. These
ensure products stay safe under normal use and foreseeable misuse, and must be applied
together with the specific standards in the IEC 60335 series tailored to each appliance
type.

## Test Coverage

- **Basic safety** — accessibility, heating, leakage current and dielectric strength,
  waterproofing
- **Electromagnetic compatibility** — electrostatic discharge, fast transients, surges and
  related assessments
- **Mechanical and material** — mechanical strength, corrosion resistance, flame resistance
- **Specialised** — flexing tests, salt spray tests, drop tests

LISUN provides one-stop testing support for manufacturers and laboratories, helping
household appliances comply with global safety regulations and enter markets smoothly.
