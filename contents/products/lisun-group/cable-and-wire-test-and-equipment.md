# Cable and Wire Test and Equipment

## Overview

LISUN's cable and wire test solutions verify compliance with international safety and
performance standards, covering rubber-insulated cables, PVC-insulated cables, medium and
high-voltage cables, and communication cables.

## Test Coverage

- **Electrical performance** — insulation resistance, dielectric strength, partial
  discharge, capacitance measurement
- **Mechanical performance** — flexing, static flexibility, wear resistance, tensile
  strength
- **Environmental adaptability** — temperature and humidity cycling, thermal shock, ozone
  resistance, salt spray corrosion
- **Safety performance** — flame retardance, resistance to heat, pressure tests at high
  temperature

Advanced automated test systems streamline the process, ensure precise data collection and
analysis, and help manufacturers and laboratories verify compliance.

## Standards Covered

| Part | Standard | Scope |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | **IEC 60245-1:2008** | Rubber insulated cables, rated voltages up to and including 450/750 V — general requirements. Durability, insulation, mechanical properties, flexing |
| 2 | **IEC 60227-1:2007** | PVC insulated cables up to 450/750 V — general requirements. Flame retardance, tensile strength, heat shock |
| 3 | **IEC 60502-1:2021** | Power cables with extruded insulation, 1 kV (Um = 1.2 kV) to 30 kV (Um = 36 kV) — Part 1: cables for 1 kV and 3 kV. Insulation resistance, high-temperature pressure, ozone resistance |
| 4 | **IEC 60840:2011** | Power cables with extruded insulation above 30 kV (Um = 36 kV) up to 150 kV (Um = 170 kV). Water penetration, lightning impulse voltage, bending |
| 5 | **IEC 60189-1:2018** | Low-frequency cables and wires with PVC insulation and sheath — general test and measuring methods. Tensile strength, insulation resistance, mutual capacitance |
| 6 | **IEC 61196-1:2005** | Mineral insulated cables and terminations, rated voltage not exceeding 750 V — Part 1: cables |
| 7 | **EN 50305:2002** | Railway applications — rolling stock cables with special fire performance — test methods |
| 8 | **UL 83:2017** | Thermoplastic-insulated wires and cables |
| 9 | **UL 44:2021** | Thermoset-insulated wires and cables |
