# Luminaire Test and Equipment

## Overview

LISUN's indoor and outdoor LED luminaire and LED fixture test solutions cover the key
international standards, providing support for testing the safety, photometric, chromatic
and environmental adaptability performance of LED products.

The solutions cover all critical test items in those standards, with equipment for:

- **Safety performance** — protection against electric shock, insulation resistance,
  resistance to tracking
- **Photometric and chromatic** — luminous flux, luminous intensity distribution, colour
  temperature, colour rendering index
- **Energy efficiency** — luminous efficacy and power factor
- **Environmental adaptability** — high and low temperature cycling, damp heat ageing,
  dust and water resistance, salt spray corrosion

This one-stop testing support helps manufacturers and laboratories ensure LED luminaires
comply with international standards and operate safely and reliably across indoor and
outdoor scenarios.

| --- | --- | --- |

| 3 | **CIE 121:1996** | The Photometry and Goniophotometry of Luminaires. Luminous intensity distribution and glare control |

| 5 | **IS 16104:2012** | DC or AC supplied electronic control gear for LED modules — performance requirements |
