# Headphone, Earbud and Smart Speaker Test

## Overview

Whether on a flight, in the office or out on a jog, people spend a lot of time wearing
speakers on or in their ears — and demand a lot from them. Big hi-fi audio quality is
expected in a very small package.

None of that performance comes easily. Engineers designing on-ear and in-ear audio products
know that measuring the audio quality of headphones, earbuds and headsets presents unique
challenges. From R&D to the manufacturing line, the selection of measurement strategies and
tools is critical to a successful product design.

## Wireless and Bluetooth Testing

| **APx Bluetooth 5** | Third-generation module delivering Bluetooth LE Audio. Supports Bluetooth specification v5.4, with Unicast Voice (transmit and receive), Unicast Music (transmit) and Auracast (broadcast transmit) |
| **APx Bluetooth Duo** | Wider selection of A2DP codecs, faster pair and connection times, more features within APx500 software, and improved RF shielding for production test environments. Supports Bluetooth core specification v4.2, HFP v1.7, HSP v1.2, AVRCP v1.4 and A2DP v1.3 |

Both modules are available options for B Series and legacy APx555, APx52x and APx58x
analysers. Configuration requires APx500 software v4.5 or later.

## Open-Loop Test Techniques

USB headsets are tested with open-loop techniques, measuring the frequency response
magnitude of a headset's microphone and earphones using the log-swept sine (chirp) method.

## Standards

**EN 50332** — headphone testing. Audio Precision publishes a demonstration of testing
headphones to EN 50332 using an APx analyser and APx500 measurement software.
