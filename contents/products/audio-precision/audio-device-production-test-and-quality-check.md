# Audio Device Production Test and Quality Check

## Overview

Audio Precision provides solutions for audio production test and measurement aimed at
minimising the cost of production testing without sacrificing quality.

From measurement performance — amplitude accuracy, frequency response flatness, and
residual noise and distortion — to out-of-the-box usability, Audio Precision's solutions
have been the standard for decades and remain the reference for most other audio
measurement systems, from R&D through to the production line.

## What Gets Tested

### Electronic Audio Devices

Power amplifiers, headphone amplifiers, DACs and ADCs.

### Acoustic Devices

Speakers, microphones, earphones and hearing aids.

## Automation and Production Test Features

The APx500 measurement software supports the creation of automated production test
sequences, with particular strength in electro-acoustic test. Capabilities include:

- Built-in test sequencing
- API-driven customisation
- Golden units (reference devices)
- Input equalisation
- Multiple sequences in a single project
- A dedicated Production Test mode

Together these let manufacturers replace subjective listening tests with fast, objective,
repeatable automated measurements.

**High Speed Tester (HST)** is a fast, accurate and easy-to-operate audio test application
suited to high-speed production test and to testing transmission quality across a
broadcast network.
