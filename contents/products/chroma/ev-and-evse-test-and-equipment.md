# EV and EVSE Test and Equipment

## Overview

Chroma provides test systems spanning the electric vehicle ecosystem — power conversion,
charging infrastructure and battery validation — covering design verification, functional
test and production line requirements.

## Test Applications

### EV Power Conversion

- Onboard chargers (OBC)
- DC-DC converters
- Motor drivers
- AC/DC EV supply equipment (EVSE)

### EV Supply Equipment (EVSE)

- Charging compatibility validation
- Protocol compliance
- Multi-coupler DC fast charging systems

### Battery

- Cell, module and pack validation
- Formation and reliability testing
- End-of-line verification
- Regenerative charge/discharge cycles

### Electrical Safety

- Leakage current and insulation resistance
- Hipot testing up to 600 V
- Partial discharge detection
- Ground continuity testing

### Motor and Wound Component

- Corona discharge detection
- Insulation quality assessment
- Turn-to-turn fault detection

## Capabilities

- Real-time hardware and software integration on an open architecture
- Regenerative discharge recycling energy to the grid or to other system channels
- Multi-channel configuration with upgrade flexibility
- Statistical analytical reporting for design review
- Low-latency multi-device data integration
- Recording and playback of real charger scenarios
- Support for ±400 V and 800 V HVDC architectures

- **GB/T** — Chinese EV charging standard
