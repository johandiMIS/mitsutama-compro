# Battery Test and Equipment

## Overview

Chroma provides high-precision, integrated battery cycling and energy storage test
solutions designed for lithium-ion and other battery chemistries. The platforms cover
requirements from R&D through to end-of-line production, and use regenerative discharge
systems that recycle energy back to other channels or to the grid.

## Coverage Across the Battery Value Chain

| Level | Scope |
| --- | --- |
| Cell | Formation, reliability testing, cell simulation |
| Module | Module cycling and validation |
| Pack | Pack-level validation and maintenance |
| BMS | Battery management system hardware-in-the-loop |
| Production | End-of-line (EOL) verification |

**Measured parameters:** charge rate, discharge rate, state of charge (SOC), state of
health (SOH), depth of discharge (DoD), voltage, current, temperature and power.

## Core Capabilities

- High-frequency sampling at **50 kHz** to avoid cumulative capacity calculation errors
- Voltage measurement accuracy to **±0.02 % of full scale**
- Fast current transitions of **10 ms–50 ms** between charge and discharge modes
- Regenerative efficiency exceeding **90 %**
- Multi-layered hardware and software safety protection
- Integration with CANbus, environmental chambers and data loggers
- Non-volatile memory for data recovery
