# Power Electronic Test and Equipment

## Overview

Chroma power electronics test solutions cover the full spread of power conversion
development and manufacturing — from bench-level R&D through to automated production
lines. The instrument range spans AC power sources, DC power supplies, DC and AC
electronic loads, digital power meters, electrical safety analysers and complete
automatic test systems, all designed for power input/output terminal tests and dynamic
simulation.

Chroma's power electronics test solutions are applied across information technology,
communications, aerospace and national defence, as well as in energy-efficient products
such as hybrid automobiles, LED luminaire devices, and solar and fuel cell systems.

## What It Tests

The systems are used to test:

- PC, server and telecom power supplies
- Adapters and chargers
- Backlight inverters and LED power drivers
- Uninterruptible power supplies (UPS)
- PV inverters
- EV chargers
- Battery packs

### Programmable AC Sources — 500 VA to 300 kVA

The `61500` Series delivers 500 VA to 60 kVA at 0–350 Vac in single- or three-phase
configuration, with low-distortion output waveforms, power line disturbance simulation,
harmonic and inter-harmonic synthesis, and high output current crest factor for inrush
current testing. Parallel operation extends output to 300 kVA.

Standards support includes IEC 61000-4-11, IEC 61000-4-14 and IEC 61000-4-28 voltage
dip and frequency variation simulation, plus a harmonics and inter-harmonics waveform
synthesiser for IEC 61000-4-13 testing.

Entry models: `61501` (500 VA), `61502` (1000 VA), `61503` (1500 VA), `61504` (2000 VA) —
all 300 V, DC and 15 Hz–1 kHz, single phase, 3U.

The `61800` Series adds a regenerative grid simulator and optional regenerative AC load.

### Digital Power Meters — 1 to 4 Channels

The `66203` / `66204` (multi-channel) and `66205` (single channel) meet ENERGY STAR,
IEC 62301, ErP ecodesign and SPEC POWER measurement requirements. The 66203/66204 support
1P2W, 1P3W, 3P3W, 3P4W and 3V3A wiring configurations; the 66205 adds a Smart Range
function for seamless power measurement with power integration under auto-range mode.
Ultra-high-precision DCCT options (`A662017` / `A662018`) and a DCCT power adapter
(`A662019`) are available.
