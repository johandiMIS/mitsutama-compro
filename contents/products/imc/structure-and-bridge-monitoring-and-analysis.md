# Structure and Bridge Monitoring and Analysis

## Overview

imc measurement systems monitor structural health and ensure the safety of civil
infrastructure through extended inspection and monitoring programmes. The problem they
address is direct: oscillations and vibrations caused by environmental impact, traffic or
structural design can lead to cracks and breaks in concrete and steel structures.

## Applications

### Bridge Monitoring

- Real-time and long-term oscillation measurement
- Decentralised modular technology for improved signal quality
- Automated remote monitoring with data transfer
- Structural health assessment and early failure detection

### Civil Engineering Structures

- Vibration and oscillation monitoring
- Thermal measurement for environmental assessment
- Acoustic monitoring
- Energy consumption analysis in buildings
- Long-term structural surveillance

## Capabilities

The platform lets engineers systematically monitor such constructions and provide
well-founded information on structural health, supporting both preventive maintenance and
efficient emergency response.

Measurement is performed close to the sensor using decentralised modular technology, which
improves signal quality over long cable runs typical of bridge and building
instrumentation.

## Why It Matters

- Early recognition of structural failures
- Safe, efficient maintenance execution
- Improved signal quality through distributed architecture
- Centralised data management and analysis
