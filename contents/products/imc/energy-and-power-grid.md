# Energy and Power Grid

## Overview

imc works with leading companies in the energy sector to offer efficient, turnkey
measurement and monitoring solutions for wind turbines, power plants and energy networks.

## Applications

- Prototype and component testing during development
- Power curve measurements for efficiency optimisation
- Load measurements to verify safety, stability and durability
- Grid and supply quality assessment
- Grid integration monitoring

**Industry focus:** onshore and offshore wind turbines, power plants, power distribution
networks and renewable energy systems.

## Measured Quantities

The platform measures all physically defined variables on the power network — frequency,
voltage spikes, voltage fluctuations, harmonics, flicker and signal frequencies — together
with all output power and performance parameters.

## Capabilities

Systems are robust and fail-safe, durable and self-sufficient, modular and decentralised.
They operate PC-independently and work reliably where normal electronics fail.

- GPS and network synchronisation across multiple measurement points
- Direct integration with plant data systems
- Integrated signal conditioning and A/D conversion
- Real-time computation and data storage
- Remote, independent operation

## Standards Referenced

- **EN 50160** — voltage characteristics of electricity supplied by public networks
- **IEC 61400-7, -15, -21, -30** — wind turbine measurement standards
- **IEC 61400-21** — grid integration

## Why It Matters

Tailored measurement solutions built on long-term experience in prototype, component,
structural and power quality testing — while maintaining operational independence and
reliability in demanding environmental conditions.
