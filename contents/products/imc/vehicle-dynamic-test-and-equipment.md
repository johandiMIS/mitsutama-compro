# Vehicle Dynamic Test and Equipment

## Overview

imc driving dynamics testing is a precision automotive measurement platform for evaluating
vehicle performance across acceleration, steering, chassis response and dynamic behaviour.
With electrification and autonomous systems, traditional methods are no longer enough —
manufacturers need advanced measurement systems that capture real-time insight into how a
vehicle actually behaves.

## Test Applications and Manoeuvres

- Safety validation, including the elk test
- ADAS and automated driving control loop evaluation
- Winter testing under extreme weather
- Stop-and-go urban durability scenarios
- High-speed performance validation
- Component prototype and quality assurance testing
- Misuse and crash testing
- Over-the-air update verification

## What Gets Measured

### Forces, Torques and Dynamics

- Six-component wheel forces
- Wheel torque
- Steering effort
- Acceleration and braking dynamics
- Suspension and shock absorber response

### Acquisition Channels

Wheel force and torque, CAN / FlexRay / Ethernet vehicle bus data, strain gauge inputs,
temperature, voltage and current sensors, plus telemetry options — the modular Dx system,
inductive MTP-NT and radio CTP-NT-Rotate.
