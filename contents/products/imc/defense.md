# Defense

## Overview

Axiometrix Solutions — the family comprising imc Test & Measurement, GRAS Sound &
Vibration and Audio Precision — provides integrated precision measurement systems for
defence applications. The solution combines data acquisition hardware, acoustic sensors,
vibration measurement equipment and analysis software to address performance, reliability
and safety requirements across military platforms and systems.

## What Gets Measured

### Acoustic

- Noise levels and acoustic signatures
- Speech intelligibility in communication systems
- Hearing protection effectiveness
- Blast and ordnance acoustic characteristics
- Source localisation and identification

### Mechanical

- Vibration and shock response
- Structural dynamics
- Durability under extreme conditions
- Load testing on vehicle platforms
- Torsional vibration analysis

## Application Areas

| Domain | Scope |
| --- | --- |
| Air force | Aircraft, helicopters, UAVs, missiles, space systems |
| Ground platforms | Armoured vehicles, tactical systems, artillery, weapons testing |
| Naval platforms | Vessels, subsystems, maritime installations |
| Soldier systems | Headsets, radios, hearing protection, tactical audio |
| Test labs and qualification | R&D, qualification, production and field deployment facilities |

Further applications include acoustic threat detection, battlefield acoustics and
mission-critical measurement systems.

## Capabilities

- Modular, scalable measurement system architecture
- Field-deployable data acquisition with telemetry options
- Real-time signal analysis and data processing
- High-speed and low-speed measurement configurations
- Rugged, environmentally hardened equipment for extreme conditions
- Remote data transfer and monitoring
- Integrated component and endurance test stands

## Key Equipment

### Data Acquisition

CRONOS series (compact, rack-based, rugged and high-speed variants), ARGUSfit (miniature),
EOS (high-speed), BUSDAQflex (vehicle-bus logging), and telemetry solutions — Dx modular,
MTP-NT, CTP-NT-Rotate, T1 and the TEL1 series.

### Software

| Package | Purpose |
| --- | --- |
| FAMOS | Test data analysis, with AI enhancements in the 2026 edition |
| imc STUDIO | Measurement and control operation |
| imc WAVE | Live NVH analysis |
| imc LINK | Remote data transfer |
| imc Online FAMOS | Real-time analysis |

### Acoustic and Specialised Sensors

GRAS precision microphone and accelerometer arrays, Audio Precision analysers for
electroacoustic testing, imc signal conditioning and transducers, plus Wheel Force
Transducers (WFT, six-component), Wheel Torque Transducers (WTT) and CLSx steering effort
sensors.

## Standards and Accreditation

- **MIL-STD-810** — environmental engineering and durability testing
- **ISO/IEC 17025:2017** — accredited calibration services
- ANSI/IEEE measurement standards
- RoHS environmental and material compliance
- DANAK (Danish accreditation) and JOSCAR (Joint Supply Chain Accreditation Register)
- Traceable calibration to national metrology institutes (DFM)
