# Train NVH Monitoring and Analysis

## Overview

imc has developed data acquisition and measurement systems for rail vehicles and
infrastructure since 1996, supporting development, prototype testing, in-service
measurement and track infrastructure evaluation across high-speed trains, streetcars and
transit systems.

## Applications

### Noise and Vibration

- Sound emission testing to **ISO 3095** (external rail vehicle noise)
- Interior noise measurement to **ISO 3381**
- Torsional vibration analysis of wheelsets
- Pantograph testing and measurement

### Dynamic Load and Force

- Wheel-rail force measurement during validation
- Wheel load monitoring at trackside checkpoints
- Dynamic load testing on subway and tram wheelsets
- Brake system testing and validation

### Structural and Safety

- Wagon body structural analysis
- Derailment testing
- Bogie maintenance and inspection
- Real-time torsional vibration limiting systems (TVLS)

### Commissioning and Monitoring

- New vehicle acceptance measurements
- Stationary wheel load checkpoints
- Flat spot detection on wheels
- Axle load overrun detection

## Capabilities

- Synchronous recording of thousands of channels
- Decentralised, distributed measurement architecture
- Direct bus system integration, including MVB bus
- Durability in harsh environments — splash water, temperature extremes,
  electromagnetic interference
- TEDS automatic calibration reading
- Real-time data analysis and computation
- Networked, synchronised operation

## Key Equipment

### Data Acquisition

ARGUSfit, CRONOSflex, CRONOScompact, CRONOS-XT, CRONOS-SL, C-SERIES, SPARTAN, EOS,
BUSDAQflex and CANSAS.

## Standards Referenced

- ISO 3095 — acoustics, measurement of noise emitted by railbound vehicles
- ISO 3381 — acoustics, measurement of noise inside railbound vehicles

## Reference Users

Alstom, Bombardier, Siemens, Deutsche Bahn, SBB, CAF, Stadler, and infrastructure testing
organisations including TÜV SÜD, Interfleet and NedTrain.
