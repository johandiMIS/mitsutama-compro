# Aeroplanes NVH and Analysis

## Overview

imc provides integrated data acquisition and analysis for aerospace testing, supporting
aircraft certification, validation and performance evaluation across multiple test phases.
Avionics testing ensures passenger safety and sustains the future of the aviation
industry.

## Applications

### Flight Test Instrumentation (FTI)

- Records analogue sensor data from hundreds to thousands of measurement channels
- Captures strain gauge, temperature, vibration, force, pressure, displacement, angle and
  flow measurements
- Synchronously records digital avionics data via **ARINC 429**, **ARINC 664** and
  **MIL-STD-1553B**
- Enables real-time telemetry transmission and ground station monitoring per **IRIG 106**

### eVTOL Testing

- Structural, powertrain, electrical and acoustic validation
- High-speed rotating component measurement
- Harsh environment data acquisition
- Multi-source synchronised collection

### Ground and Structural Testing

- Airframe, wing, rotor blade and landing gear analysis
- Strain and stress measurement for static, fatigue and endurance testing
- Composite material characterisation
- Turbine engine instrumentation
- Hardware-in-the-loop (HiL) solutions

## Capabilities

- Highly accurate and reliable data acquisition
- Real-time analysis, monitoring and data reduction
- Decentralised, distributed measurement systems
- TEDS-automated sensor integration
- Anti-aliasing analogue inputs
- Software-configurable across all systems

## Key Equipment

### Data Acquisition

CRONOS series (modular and rugged variants), ARGUSfit, C-SERIES and EOS for high-speed
acquisition.

### Software

FAMOS (analysis), imc STUDIO (measurement and control), imc WAVE (NVH) and imc LINK
(remote data transfer).

### Sensors and Test Stands

Wheel force transducers, telemetry systems (Dx, MTP-NT, CTP-NT), plus component,
endurance and retrofit test stand configurations.

## Standards Referenced

- ARINC 429 and ARINC 664 — avionics data buses
- MIL-STD-1553B — military avionics data bus
- IRIG 106 — telemetry standards

## Why It Matters

A one-stop shop for efficient measurement solutions throughout the whole testing cycle —
rapid, reliable results via integrated hardware and software, with user-specific
extensions and broad bus data system support.
