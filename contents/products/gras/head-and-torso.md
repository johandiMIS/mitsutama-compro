# Head and Torso

## Overview

Audio design has become a vital part of developing everyday products — automobiles,
computers, hearing aids, mobile phones. KEMAR, the GRAS head and torso simulator (HATS),
is the recognised industry standard for in-situ anthropomorphic testing.

KEMAR was originally invented in collaboration with the audiological industry for hearing
aid development and remains the de facto standard there. Since then its use has spread
into telecommunications, hearing protection testing, automotive development and beyond.

## What Makes KEMAR Different

The major difference between KEMAR and other head and torso simulators on the market is
that KEMAR is built on large statistical research of the average human body — meaning it
has the same acoustical properties as an average human, including distinct facial
features.

## Recognised Fields of Use

- Telecommunications
- Hearing conservation
- Noise abatement
- Sound recording and sound-quality evaluation

## Configuration System

KEMAR has many configuration possibilities. To simplify ordering, GRAS offers a number of
preconfigured variants:

| Base | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| 45BB-X | Without mouth simulator |
| 45BC-X | With mouth simulator |

All factory-configured KEMAR are available in both LEMO (X = 1, 3, 5, 7) and CCP
(X = 2, 4, 6, 8) versions. Non-configured KEMAR (`45BB`, `45BC`) are available for
customers who own earlier KEMAR units (`45BA`, `45BM`) and wish to reuse configuration
parts — 100 % compatibility. Optional accessories make it easy to change configuration.

## Standards

KEMAR meets international standards specified by ISO, IEC and ANSI. Depending on
configuration:

- **ANSI S3.36** and **ANSI S3.25**
- **IEC 60318-4** (formerly 60711) — occluded-ear simulator
- **IEC 60318-7** (formerly 60959) — head and torso simulator
- **ITU-T Rec. P.57** Type 3.3 — artificial ears
- **ITU-T Rec. P.58** — artificial heads

Low-noise headset variants are *based on* IEC 60318-4 and 60318-7.
