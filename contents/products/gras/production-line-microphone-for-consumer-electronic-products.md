# Production Line Microphone for Consumer Electronic Products

## Overview

Production line testing ensures that finished products match the target quality set by R&D
and that no sub-standard units reach the market.

Production line microphones must deliver repeatable, precise measurements every day so
that products failing specification are not delivered to end users. Because they are
usually needed in far greater numbers than in an R&D lab, they must also fit the budget.
GRAS production line microphones provide the best combination of cost and quality.

## EQset™ Technology

EQset uses digital signal processing to maintain consistent sensitivity and a flat
frequency response across all frequencies within strict tolerances. Unlike other
production microphones, the EQset models incorporate a high-quality condenser measurement
microphone capsule, giving high precision and exceptional environmental stability.

## Choosing Between Variants

- **40PO-H vs 40PO-L** — the 40PO-H suits high sound pressure level measurement; the
  40PO-L, with higher sensitivity, suits lower SPL measurement
- **40PM vs 40PM-1** — the 40PM-1 extends the upper dynamic range from 120 dB to 125 dB
  and is the higher-precision option
- **40PP-10 vs 40PP-10-S1** — the S1 trades sensitivity for a 142 dB upper limit
